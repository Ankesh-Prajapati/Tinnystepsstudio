Our story

It always feels like home here

Tinyy Steps Studio was built on one idea: the best baby photos happen when nobody feels like they're in a studio at all. Soft cushions, warm light, gentle music — we slow everything down until your little one settles in.

We photograph the quiet, in-between moments — a mother's hand around tiny fingers, a first birthday cake smash, a toddler's shy giggle — and shape every session around what your family actually wants to remember.

  • A calm, home-styled space — no harsh lights, no rush
  • Gentle with newborns and nervous toddlers alike
  • Every session is thoughtfully planned around your family
